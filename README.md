## 🏢 About the Company

**Britannia Industries Limited** (NSE: BRITANNIA | BSE: 500825) is one of India's oldest and most iconic FMCG companies, founded in 1892 and headquartered in Bengaluru. A subsidiary of the Wadia Group, Britannia is the market leader in the Indian biscuits segment with brands like **Good Day, NutriChoice, Marie Gold, Tiger, Milk Bikis and 50-50**.

-  Exports to **60+ countries**
-  Manufacturing plants across **6+ states**
-  Products across **Biscuits, Bread, Cakes, Rusk & Dairy**
